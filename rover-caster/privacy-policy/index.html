Privacy Policy — Rover Caster

Developer: Evan Studio · Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Rover Caster game ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Rover Caster is published by Evan Studio ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, selected car, sound setting and purchase status) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

2. Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect information as described in their own policies:

ServicePurposeInformation it may process

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Consent: if you are shown a consent form, you can change your choice later by reinstalling the app.
  • Remove ads: the VIP subscription or the Remove Ads purchase turns off banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos are always optional.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in the Google Play Store.

4. Children

Rover Caster is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security and retention

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own systems, we have nothing to retain. Data handled by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
