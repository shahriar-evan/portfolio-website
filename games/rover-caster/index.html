// How to play

Three steps, endless ideas

Look

Study the gap, the wall or the saw between your car and the flag.

Draw

Drag your finger to draw a single chalk line — a bridge, a ramp or a shield.

Drive

Let go. The line drops with real physics and the car drives. Use less chalk for 3 stars.

// Features

Small game, lots inside

Real physics

Box2D simulation — lines fall, tilt and balance, so every solution is your own.

300+ levels

Hand-made puzzles, then endless generated levels — every one tested to be solvable.

Traps & obstacles

Spinning and moving saws, deep pits, tall cliffs and floating islands.

6 cars to unlock

Taxi, Patrol, Bubble Van, Trail Jeep, Rocket Racer and the classic buggy.

Hints & skips

Stuck? See where to draw, or skip the level with coins or a short video.

Chalkboard vibe

Hand-drawn look, relaxing music and sound effects — all made from code.
